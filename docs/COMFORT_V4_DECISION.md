# AION Service CRM Comfort v4 — Owner-approved direction

Date: 2026-08-01

Status: APPROVED PROTOTYPE DIRECTION / IMPLEMENTED AS DEMO / NOT PRODUCT-INTEGRATED

## Goal

Make the commercial UI more comfortable for a real service shift: less vertical air, faster orientation, active orders higher on the screen, clearer next action, and equal usability in dark and light themes.

## Approved changes

- preserve AION Commercial UI visual DNA;
- compact the top shell and greeting area;
- make EvE smaller and action-oriented;
- place the working queue before secondary modules;
- keep New Repair as the dominant action;
- add quick actions for search and issuance;
- make the current order more compact and operational;
- reduce bottom dock height while preserving the central repair action;
- support dark/light theme persistence;
- keep reduced-motion support;
- preserve AION Basic as a later rendering profile, not a separate CRM.

## Non-goals

- no real API, database, customer data, payments, authentication, or production QR;
- no claim of proven usability, speed, commercial conversion, or old-device compatibility;
- no silent automation of price, signature, QC, payment, or device issuance.

## Next gate

Physical iPhone review of Comfort v4 in both themes, followed by a focused Express Intake comfort pass and Current Order workspace pass.
