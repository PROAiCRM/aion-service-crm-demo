# AION Repair Offer — Pilot Guide v0.2

## Fast test on one phone

1. Open `repair-offer-v0-2.html`.
2. Tap **Run ready example** or create a new proposal.
3. Open the proposal and tap **Open as customer**.
4. Select an option, confirm the pilot consent, and approve.
5. Return to the service panel and verify the status and Action Receipt.
6. Convert the approved proposal into a local repair-order draft.

## Cross-device test

### Service phone or computer

1. Create a proposal.
2. Tap **Share link**.
3. Send the link to the second phone.

### Customer phone

1. Open the link.
2. Choose an option.
3. Approve, ask a question, or decline.
4. Tap **Share decision** and send the response link back to the service.

### Service phone or computer

1. Open the returned response link on the same browser where the proposal was created.
2. Confirm that the decision is imported and the status changes.

## Test data rule

Use only synthetic labels such as “Alexey demo”, “Client 24”, or “Test order”. Do not enter a real phone number, surname, address, serial number, Apple ID, password, or payment data.

## What to record

For every real pilot interaction, record outside the public demo:

- creation time;
- whether the customer opened the link;
- whether the customer understood the differences;
- decision type;
- agreed amount;
- whether the response-link return step caused difficulty;
- whether the repair was actually completed;
- employee and customer comments.
